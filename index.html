01 — THE BIG PICTURE

A nine-year story of speed and injury

Speeding-related injuries remained a significant issue across India between 2008 and 2016. The data shows that the number of people injured in accidents caused by exceeding the lawful speed limit changed considerably over the nine-year period.

The national figures did not follow a steady pattern. After falling in the early years, the numbers began increasing again and reached their highest point in 2016.

02 — NATIONAL TREND

The numbers moved up and down — before reaching a peak

The national figures fluctuated throughout the period. The number fell from 2008 to 2009, rose slightly in 2010, declined again, and eventually climbed to its highest point in 2016.

07 — WHAT THE DATA TELLS US

The numbers show where and when.

But the dataset does not explain why these changes happened. It presents a mixed picture: some states experienced sharp increases while others recorded considerable declines.

What remains clear from the nine-year data is that injuries linked to exceeding the lawful speed limit continued to be a significant issue, with the national figure reaching its highest point in 2016.
